# Attribution for the files in this folder

The PET images in this folder (stored in `data/*/PT/`) are derived from the following publicly‑available data sets hosted on The Cancer Imaging Archive (TCIA) 
and are distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0) licence (see LICENSE.txt).

**Original sources:**
- Kinahan, P., Muzi, M., Bialecki, B., Herman, B., & Coombs, L. (2019). Data from the ACRIN 6668 Trial NSCLC-FDG-PET (Version 2) [Data set]. The Cancer Imaging Archive. https://doi.org/10.7937/tcia.2019.30ilqfcl
- Kinahan, P., Muzi, M., Bialecki, B., & Coombs, L. (2017). Data from ACRIN-FLT-Breast (Version 2) [Data set]. The Cancer Imaging Archive. DOI: https://doi.org/10.7937/K9/TCIA.2017.ol20zmxg
- Li, X., Abramson, R. G., Arlinghaus, L. R., Chakravarthy, A. B., Abramson, V. G., Sanders, M., & Yankeelov, T. E. (2016). Data From QIN-BREAST (Version 2) [Data set]. The Cancer Imaging Archive. https://doi.org/10.7937/K9/TCIA.2016.21JUEBH0
- Vallières, Martin, Freeman, Carolyn R., Skamene, Sonia R., & El Naqa, Issam. (2015). A radiomics model from joint FDG-PET and MRI texture features for the prediction of lung metastases in soft-tissue sarcomas of the extremities (Soft-tissue-Sarcoma) [Dataset]. The Cancer Imaging Archive. http://doi.org/10.7937/K9/TCIA.2015.7GO2GSKS
- Zuley, M. L., Jarosz, R., Drake, B. F., Rancilio, D., Klim, A., Rieger-Christ, K., & Lemmerman, J. (2016). The Cancer Genome Atlas Prostate Adenocarcinoma Collection (TCGA-PRAD) (Version 4) [Data set]. The Cancer Imaging Archive. https://doi.org/10.7937/K9/TCIA.2016.YXOGLM4Y

Data for NSCLC Radiogenomics (Version 4) [Data set]. The Cancer Imaging Archive. https://doi.org/10.7937/K9/TCIA.2017.7hs46erv

**Modifications**

No modifications were applied to the original PET images.

Masks (stored in `data/*/mask/`), each including one spherical ROI for SUV computation, are released under CC BY 3.0 licence (see LICENSE.txt). 
They were newly generated for each PET series using LIFEx v25.06.1 (https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN‑18‑0125) and for compatibility purposes, the headers were edited using SimpleITK (https://doi.org/10.3389/fninf.2013.00045).

**How to cite**

When you reuse any PET images from this folder, please cite the original data set(s) as shown above. 

If you use other files (i.e., the masks or contours), do not forget to cite our repository: https://github.com/oncoray/suv_computation.
